03 / Method

How I work

The habits underneath every entry above.

01 / Evidence

I prefer work that leaves artefacts: tests, logs, plots, traces and procedures someone else can re-run. A claim without an artefact is an opinion.

02 / Interfaces

Most failures live at interfaces: joints and load paths, tolerances, thermal boundaries, packet formats, timing, frame conventions and fault handling. That is where I spend my attention.

03 / Honesty

Blocked work is stated as blocked, design targets are labelled as targets, and archived projects keep their closeouts. Hiding the gaps costs more than it saves.
