ORBITALIS-RPO: Spacecraft Rendezvous & Proximity Operations

Relative navigation · constrained guidance · 6-DOF · autonomy · C++20 verification

What it does

Software-in-the-loop spacecraft inspection and RPO spanning relative dynamics, asynchronous navigation, constrained guidance, quaternion 6-DOF propagation, autonomy and retreat logic.

Evidence

200 frozen cases retained 132 full missions, 62 safe abort-retreats, six timeouts and zero truth-level keep-out violations. The C++20 core passed 96/96 frozen Python-reference vectors.

AeroFrame-DT: Structural Substantiation & Pylon Attachment Trade Study

Parametric CAD · hand calculations · nonlinear FEA · allowables · fatigue / damage tolerance

Engineering challenge

Carry a synthetic pylon-to-wingbox attachment fitting from controlled geometry and loads through hand analysis, nonlinear FEA, material allowables, fatigue, damage tolerance and verification.

Released result

The final 7050-T7451 configuration closed 18/18 requirements with a +0.151 governing margin. FE force equilibrium closed to 0.006%, and a five-variant e/D trade preserved the failure-mode transition and unresolved limits.
