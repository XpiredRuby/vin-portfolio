GHOST: GPS-Denied Occlusion-Survivable Tracker

Dual-filter visual-inertial estimation on embedded Linux

Overview

A vehicle that loses GNSS still has to know where it is, and where a manoeuvring target is, through seconds-long visual occlusions, on Raspberry Pi-class compute. GHOST separates those two problems into two filters that fail independently.

Current state

Software layer complete and committed: ESKF, IMM tracker, NIS consistency validation and CI. Hardware bring-up is blocked on an IMX296 driver/kernel issue on Ubuntu 22.04 / documented openly rather than hidden.

Rocket Landing GNC Simulation

6-DOF booster descent · thrust-vector control · 500-run Monte Carlo campaign

Overview

A 6-DOF propulsive-landing simulator with rigid-body dynamics, aerodynamic and aerothermal modelling, mass depletion and gimballed thrust, wrapped in a Monte Carlo harness that dispersed winds, initial state and sensor error across 500 seeded trials.

Result

Sub-2.0 m landing accuracy across the dispersed set, with the failure tail traced to a specific wind-shear-plus-late-ignition combination rather than to controller gain margin.

AeroFrame-MD11: CAD Assembly & Structural Analysis

24-part parametric assembly · static / thermal / modal FEA · release drawing package

Overview

A full parametric CAD assembly of an MD-11-class airframe section with GD&T, taken through static, thermal and modal finite element analysis at 2.5 g limit manoeuvre loading, and closed out with a manufacturing-release drawing package.

Result

Three stress concentration sites identified, root joint, upper skin panel and attach lug, each traced to a geometry decision rather than a mesh artefact, and each with a proposed design change.

AeroFrame-DT: Pylon Attachment Trade Study

Parametric CAD · lug hand calculations · nonlinear solid FEA · decision records

Engineering challenge

Determine which geometry variable actually controls a forward pylon-to-wingbox attachment fitting, then keep the load assumptions, model evidence, and unresolved limits traceable.

Result

A five-variant e/D study located the failure-mode crossover near 1.35, confirmed elastic response scaling within 1.7%, and preserved the plastic-strain inconsistency as an open finding instead of hiding it behind a contour maximum.

AI-Powered Autonomous Interception Robot

YOLO detection · Kalman filtering · PID actuation on Raspberry Pi 4

Overview

A complete perception-to-actuation loop on embedded Linux: camera capture, neural detection, Kalman state estimation, an azimuth/elevation error law, and PWM motor drive, with the whole chain held under a 100 ms latency budget.

Result

Sub-100 ms closed-loop latency achieved. Profiling showed inference dominating the budget at roughly three quarters of the total, which is where every subsequent optimisation went.

Project KESTREL: Throw-Launch Precision Landing UAV

Vision-only EKF · Pixhawk 6C · sub-5 cm CEP design target

Overview

A hand-thrown fixed-geometry UAV designed to recover to a marked pad with sub-5 cm circular error probable using vision-only terminal guidance, no GNSS in the landing phase.

Why it is here

Not everything ships. KESTREL is kept in the register because the reason it stopped is itself an engineering result, and the vision-only terminal-guidance work fed directly into GHOST.
