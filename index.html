PRJ-01
Active development 2026 — PRESENT

GHOST — GPS-Denied Occlusion-Survivable Tracker

Dual-filter visual-inertial estimation on embedded Linux

Engineering challenge

GNSS is the first thing an adversary or an urban canyon takes away. The vehicle still has to know where it is, and where a moving target is, through seconds-long visual occlusions — on a compute budget that fits a Raspberry Pi.

Approach

Two filters with separate jobs: a 9-state error-state Kalman filter fusing 200 Hz IMU propagation with 30 Hz AprilTag pose fixes for ego-state, and an interacting-multiple-model tracker (constant-velocity / constant-turn) that coasts the target through dropouts with a χ² gate on reacquisition.

HIL verified COMPLETE

AstraSim-FSW — Flight Software HIL Verification Framework

C++17 flight segment · Python ground segment · Raspberry Pi target

Engineering challenge

Flight software claims are worthless without evidence. Building the evidence chain — commands, telemetry, fault injection, watchdog behaviour, regression — usually requires lab hardware a student cannot get near.

Approach

A complete command/telemetry stack: binary packets with CRC-16-CCITT validation and command acknowledgement, a mode manager, health monitor and watchdog, a fault injector that drops, corrupts and delays packets, YAML-driven scenarios, and a seeded Monte Carlo regression harness — all cross-built and run on an aarch64 Raspberry Pi target.

PRJ-03
Complete DEC 2025

Rocket Landing GNC Simulation

6-DOF booster descent · TVC control · 500-run Monte Carlo campaign

Engineering challenge

A propulsive landing controller that works on the nominal trajectory proves nothing. The question a reviewer asks is: what is the landing dispersion across the wind and sensor-error envelope, and what fraction of cases close?

Approach

A 6-DOF descent simulator with rigid-body dynamics, aerodynamic and aerothermal modelling, mass depletion and thrust-vector actuation, wrapped in a Monte Carlo harness that dispersed initial state, winds and sensor bias across 500 seeded trials.
