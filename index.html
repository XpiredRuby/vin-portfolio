01 / About me

I care about the part after “it works.”

I’m an Aerospace Engineering student at Texas A&M, graduating in May 2027 and continuing through the Fast Track BS/MS program. My deepest technical focus is GNC, state estimation, simulation, and flight software; my structures and systems work gives me a broader vehicle-level view of requirements, loads, interfaces, and verification.

What connects my projects is the same engineering habit: define the claim, build the system, design a test that can prove the claim wrong, and keep the negative results when they teach something useful.
