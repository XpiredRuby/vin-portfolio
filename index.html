02 / About

I care about what happens after “it works.”

My projects usually start with a physical-system question: what happens when the sensor disappears, the command is corrupted, the structure misses margin, or a clean simulation assumption reaches real hardware?

I define what I am actually claiming, build a test that can prove me wrong, and keep the negative result when it changes the engineering decision. My primary direction is GNC, state estimation, simulation and flight software; structures and systems work give me useful breadth across the vehicle.
